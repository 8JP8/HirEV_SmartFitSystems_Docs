Descrição detalhada

Generates collision-free CMM scanner paths by orbiting the part on a bounding sphere and using FOV raycasting to guarantee full surface coverage.

Strategy ════════

  1. Compute a bounding sphere (centre + radius) that englobes the mesh.
  2. Place scanner candidates on a larger concentric sphere (radius = bounding-sphere + optimal standoff) via a Fibonacci lattice for near-uniform angular distribution.
  3. Optionally filter candidates by elevation to avoid the bottom face.
  4. Run a greedy set-cover using FOV raycasting to choose the minimum subset of candidates that achieves the target surface coverage.
  5. Detect cylindrical holes and inject dedicated axial scan passes.
  6. Order all selected positions with a nearest-neighbour TSP heuristic (arc-distance on the sphere).
  7. Connect consecutive positions with great-circle interpolation so the scanner stays on the bounding sphere at all times (no part collision).

◆ DetectHoles()

List< DetectedHole > HirEV_SmartFitSystemsApp.Modules.ScanPathManager.DetectHoles ( Mesh mesh)
private

Detects cylindrical holes by finding mesh boundary loops and classifying approximately circular ones.

Algorithm ─────────

  1. Build edge → triangle adjacency. Boundary edges appear in exactly one triangle.
  2. Chain boundary edges into closed vertex loops.
  3. For each closed loop compute its centroid, best-fit normal, and radius.
  4. Accept loops whose vertex-to-centre distance variance is low (≈ circular) and whose radius exceeds the minimum threshold.
  5. Estimate hole depth by raycasting inward along the axis.

◆ GenerateMultiPartSphericalScanPaths()

List< ScanPath > HirEV_SmartFitSystemsApp.Modules.ScanPathManager.GenerateMultiPartSphericalScanPaths ( IEnumerable< CADFace > allFaces,
Mesh? combinedMesh,
ScanPathOptions options,
IEnumerable< CADHole >? knownHoles = null )

Plans scan paths for every CAD part independently, then bridges consecutive parts through the apex of their respective bounding spheres.

Bridge strategy (see diagram below):

[ last pose of part A ]
↓ SLERP arc on A's sphere (avoids bottom if configured)
[ apex of A's scanner sphere ]
↓ straight high-altitude transit
[ apex of B's scanner sphere ]
↓ SLERP arc on B's sphere (avoids bottom if configured)
[ first pose of part B ]

When ScanPathOptions.AvoidBottomFace is true the SLERP arcs are forced to travel through the upper hemisphere only (the algorithm picks the shortest SLERP that stays above the elevation limit).
