Descrição detalhada

Data model representing a detected hole feature in a 3D CAD model. Holes are identified from pairs of cylindrical/conical faces and classified by type and size.

A hole is created from two CADFace objects that form a complete hole geometry:

  • Inner and outer cylindrical surfaces
  • Top and bottom planar surfaces (entry/exit points)

Holes are automatically classified as:

  • Standard metric threads (M3, M6, etc.) based on tap drill diameter
  • Clearance holes (nominal diameter + 0.5mm)
  • Custom holes with measured dimensions

Created by HoleDetector.DetectHoles and may be manually created via HoleDetector.CreateHoleFromTwoFaces.
