Descrição detalhada

Visibility Graph Pathfinder for collision-free path planning.

Based on computational geometry algorithms from:

  • de Berg et al. "Computational Geometry: Algorithms and Applications"
  • LaValle "Planning Algorithms" (2006)

The visibility graph connects waypoints (approach points) with edges only where a straight line between them doesn't intersect obstacles. A* search then finds the shortest collision-free path.
