Descrição detalhada

RRT* (Rapidly-exploring Random Tree Star) Pathfinder for collision-free path planning.

Based on: Karaman & Frazzoli (2011) "Sampling-based algorithms for optimal motion planning"

RRT* is asymptotically optimal - it converges to the optimal path as samples increase. Used as fallback when Visibility Graph fails for complex geometries.
