◆ HoleTypeEnum

enum HirEV_SmartFitSystemsApp.Models.HoleTypeEnum

Enumeration of hole types that can be detected and classified in CAD models. Used to categorize holes for measurement strategy selection and tooling requirements.

Hole types are determined by analyzing:

  • Cylindrical face geometry (diameter, depth, axis)
  • Adjacent planar surfaces (top/bottom)
  • Conical features (countersinks)
  • Nesting relationships (counterbores)

Classification affects:

  • Measurement probe selection
  • Approach angle and retract strategy
  • Inspection point density
  • Quality control criteria
Valores de enumerações
Unknown 

Hole type could not be determined. Default state before classification.

CylindricalSimple 

Simple cylindrical hole with constant diameter. Can be through-hole or blind-hole (distinguished by depth vs part thickness). Most common hole type for bolts, dowels, and standard fasteners.

CylindricalCounterbore 

Cylindrical hole with a larger diameter counterbore at the top. Used for recessing bolt heads or nuts below the surface. Detected as nested hole configuration (smaller hole inside larger hole).

CylindricalCountersink 

Cylindrical hole with a conical countersink at the top. Used for flush-mounting flat-head screws. Detected by conical face adjacent to smaller cylindrical hole.

ConicalSimple 

Simple conical hole without cylindrical portion. Uncommon in mechanical parts; typically chamfers or tapers.

Threaded 

Hole identified as threaded based on diameter matching tap drill size. Requires thread gauge or specialized probe for internal thread inspection.

ThroughHole 

Hole that completely penetrates the part (depth ≈ local thickness). Has both entry and exit points with measurable edges.

BlindHole 

Hole that does not penetrate completely (depth < local thickness). Has entry point but ends within the material. Requires depth measurement capability.
