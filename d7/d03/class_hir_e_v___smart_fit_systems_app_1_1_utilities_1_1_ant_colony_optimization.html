Descrição detalhada

Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) algorithm for optimizing CMM measurement paths. Solves the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) to minimize total distance.

Based on: Stojadinovic et al. (2016) - "Towards an intelligent approach for CMM inspection planning" Section 3.4: ACO Optimization

Experimental results from the papers:

  • 20% shorter vs ZEISS UMM500 online programming
  • 10% shorter vs CMM Pro/ENGINEER module

Typical parameters:

  • α (alpha): 1.0 - pheromone weight
  • β (beta): 2.0 - heuristic distance weight
  • ρ (rho): 0.1 - pheromone evaporation rate
  • Q: 100.0 - pheromone deposit intensity
