Descrição detalhada

Utility for computing CMM probe kinematic chains and building Eyeshot 3D block assemblies.

Coordinate convention:

  • Head quill face is the kinematic origin (Z = 0 mm).
  • Positive Z is upward (toward the CMM bridge), negative Z is downward (toward part).
  • Axis-B pivot is at Z = PivotBOffsetZ (default –5 mm).
  • Axis-A pivot is at Z = PivotAOffsetZ (default –25 mm).
  • Ruby ball centre is at Z = –(|PivotAOffset| + TotalReachLength) when A = 0.

Tip-centric design: All entity positions are driven from the ruby ball tip location. Calling ComputeKinematics with a target tip position and approach angles solves the full chain (quill → head → B-pivot → A-pivot → probe body → stylus → tip). The head always remains vertical (upright Z-aligned).

Builds an Eyeshot Block containing the full kinematic CMM assembly with correct tip-centric positioning.

Layout (all in local block coordinates, head quill face at origin):

  • Head body: centred at origin, pointing upward, rendered vertical.
  • Probe + stylus chain: rotated about the A/B wrist pivot so the ruby tip ends up at (0, 0, –(|PivotAOffset| + TotalReach)).
  • Ruby ball: positioned exactly at the tip contact point.

double angleADeg,

static

Computes full CMM kinematic chain positions from a target tip location and wrist angles.

The head stays vertical (Z-quill axis). The probe body is rotated by Axis-A (tilt) then Axis-B (pan) about the articulating knuckle pivot. The ruby ball tip ends up at targetTipCenter .

Parâmetros
targetTipCenterDesired world position of the ruby ball centre.
angleADegAxis-A tilt angle in degrees (0 = straight down, 90 = horizontal).
angleBDegAxis-B pan angle in degrees (0 = toward +Y, CCW from above).
montageMontage definition containing pivot offsets and reach length.
