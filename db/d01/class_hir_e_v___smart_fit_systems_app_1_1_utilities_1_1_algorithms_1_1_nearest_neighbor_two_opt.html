Descrição detalhada

Provides a lightweight combination of Nearest-Neighbor seeding followed by a 2-Opt improvement pass. The heuristic is tailored for open paths (not TSP cycles) and preserves the start/end ordering produced by the seeding stage while aggressively shortening mid-segments.
