Descrição detalhada

Extracts hole axis information and generates axis-constrained probe paths for hole features. Implements the axis-only probing rule: travel along hole centerline, move radially to measurement points.

MoveToAxisEntrance

MoveAlongAxis 

Move along hole axis (descent or ascent).

MoveRadialToMeasurement 

Move radially from axis to measurement point.

MoveRadialToAxis

ExitToShell
