Descrição detalhada

Provides camera control and automation utilities for the Eyeshot 3D viewport.

Features include:

  • Automatic camera following of probe during path simulation
  • Zoom-to-fit functionality for faces, holes, and vertices
  • Smooth camera transitions and viewport management

Adjustment strategy:

EventArgs e )
private

Timer tick handler that updates camera position to follow the probe during simulation.

Parâmetros
senderEvent sender (DispatcherTimer)
eEvent arguments

Execution sequence:

  1. Searches for the probe tracking mesh on the "PROBE_TRACKING" layer
  2. Calculates the probe's center position
  3. Compares with last known position (movement threshold: 0.5mm)
  4. If probe moved significantly and is out of view, adjusts camera
