Descrição detalhada

Represents transformation and kinematic chain data for a CMM probe tool.

Tip-centric design: All positioning is defined relative to the ruby ball contact point (the tip). The kinematic chain (head → wrist → probe body → stylus) adapts to place the tip at the correct location with the correct approach angle.

Head always vertical: For Cartesian CMMs the head stays aligned with the Z-quill. Only the articulating wrist (Axis A tilt + Axis B pan) rotates the probe body.
