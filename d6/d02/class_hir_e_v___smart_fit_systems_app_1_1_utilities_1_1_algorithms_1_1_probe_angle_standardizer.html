double HirEV_SmartFitSystemsApp.Utilities.Algorithms.ProbeAngleStandardizer.A
static

Converts a continuous 3D orientation vector into discrete CMM bi-axial head angles (A, B) relative to the Eyeshot global vertical axis (+Z).

Selects the optimal standardized discrete probe angle (A, B) for a waypoint, evaluating the angular reuse threshold to avoid unnecessary head rotations, measuring angle tolerance, and base avoidance.

Parâmetros
vThe 3D direction vector.
stepADiscrete angular step for axis A (in degrees).
stepBDiscrete angular step for axis B (in degrees).
maxAMaximum permissible A angle (in degrees).
Retorna
A tuple containing discrete angles (A, B) in degrees.
