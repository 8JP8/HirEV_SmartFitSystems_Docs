◆ DmisWristAnglesMode

enum HirEV_SmartFitSystemsApp.Modules.PostProcessors.DmisWristAnglesMode

Specifies the DMIS syntax style for motorized wrist head orientation statements.

Valores de enumerações
WristAngles 

Standard wrist angle minor words: SW(PH10),'RotAngle',B,'TiltAngle',A Conforms to ANSI/DMIS 5.3 Clause 6.182 and Annex A.29.

HeadCs 

Head coordinate system minor words: HEADCS,A,B Conforms to ANSI/DMIS 5.3 Clause 6.182 and Clause 6.147.

Both 

Emits wrist angles in SNSLCT and HEADCS in PTMEAS.
