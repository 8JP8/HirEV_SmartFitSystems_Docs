Descrição detalhada

Collision Avoidance Algorithm - ACA. Detects and corrects triangle-segment intersections iteratively.

Based on: Stojadinovic et al. (2016) - "Towards an intelligent approach for CMM inspection planning" Section 3.3: ACA algorithm with iterative triangle-segment detection

Principle:

  1. Load STL model (triangles)
  2. For each trajectory segment, test intersection with triangles
  3. If a collision occurs, correct iteratively by raising points by δ (correction delta)
  4. Re-evaluate until no collisions remain
